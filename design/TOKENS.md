## Type
- English: Inter (400/500/600/700/800). Arabic: Tajawal (400/500/700/800).
- Numbers & stats: IBM Plex Mono (500/600).
- Sizes: screen title 22–24 / 800, card title 15–17 / 700, body 14, caption 12, micro 11.

## Shape & spacing

- Gaps: 8 / 10 / 12 / 14 / 16px. Icons: 1.8–2px stroke line icons (lucide-react-native).
